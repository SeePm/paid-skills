# Pinterest Ads: what to check per lens

Facts Pinterest documents publicly. Check the current Pinterest Business Help Center when something looks off; Pinterest replaced its conversion objectives recently.

## X against Y
- Objectives are awareness, consideration, leads, sales and app install. Leads is for non-transactional events (signup, lead); sales for transactional ones (add to cart, checkout). The older Conversions and Catalog sales objectives are legacy and stop being editable on April 30, 2027.
- A leads campaign can optimize for checkout, signup, lead or add to cart. Check the event matches the stage Y lives at.
- Pinterest says leads and sales campaigns perform best with 50 or more conversion events a week, and suggests starting with consideration below that.
- The conversion window (what data delivery learns from) and the attribution window (what reporting credits) are set separately. Pinterest recommends aligning them.

## Setup
- Performance+ campaigns automate bidding and targeting and create their own ad group, plus prospecting and retargeting ad groups when a catalog is used.

## Measurement
- Tag and Conversions API events deduplicate on a shared event_id and event name; Pinterest keeps the first event and drops duplicates within 48 hours. The Deduplication tab in Ads Manager shows coverage.
- Conversion insights defaults to 30 days after a click and 1 day after a view when no window is chosen. Shopify, for example, reports last click only, so it will show fewer conversions than Pinterest.

## Budget and pacing
- Performance+ uses only lifetime and average daily budgets.
